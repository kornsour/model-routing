# 2026-10-02 · exp06 Stage 0 extension: H0 not met twice, exp06 closes

Real run on the Claude track (`exp06_calibrate_ext`, subscription login,
Claude Code 2.1.285), task set `tasks/exp06/tasks_ext.jsonl` (40 tasks in four
strata, built and validated before any model saw them:
`2026-10-01-exp06-stage0-ext-taskset.md`), Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 pinned by
exact id, randomized block order, 3 trials, per-task turn caps (80 for the
long-horizon stratum, 40 elsewhere). 240 graded cells, **$88.48** list price
(Opus $58.66, Sonnet $29.83), against a $150 budget. No usage limit, outage or
budget pause. Run directory `results/exp06_calibrate_ext/20261001-195346`,
backed up.

This is the one task-set extension the v0.3 stopping rule allows
(`../extension-plan.md`). It is exploratory; nothing was registered.

## Result: 5 of 55 hard; H0 not met, for the second time

H0 is evaluated on the pooled set (the 15 first-batch tasks plus these 40) with
the protocol's definition (paper §7.1, `model_routing.dispatch.exp06`): a task
is **hard** when Opus passes at least 2 of 3 trials and Sonnet at most 1 of 3.
The bar is 10 hard tasks.

| | easy | medium | hard | unsolved | total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| first batch (2026-09-29 run) | 14 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 15 |
| A. harvested handoffs | 12 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 16 |
| B. long-horizon, multi-package | 9 | 0 | 3 | 0 | 12 |
| C. ambiguous | 4 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 6 |
| D. read-only investigation | 6 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 6 |
| **pooled** | **45** | **2** | **5** | **3** | **55** |

The two medium tasks (Sonnet 2 of 3) are `tb-cron-03` and
`ha-runner-class-01`. The unsolved tasks are `ha-archive-birchwood-05`,
`ha-verify-suite-16` (a grader defect, below) and `hc-history-csv-05`.

**5 hard of 55 against a bar of 10: H0 is not met.** By the stopping rule it
has now failed twice, and the finding stands for this class of work. No
grader decision can change it: counting every unsolved task as hard gives 8.

### The five hard tasks, inspected one by one

As for the first batch, every Sonnet failure on a hard task was read against
its grade detail, to separate capability gaps from grader strictness.

| task | Sonnet | Opus | what Sonnet got wrong | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| `hc-run-json-04` (C) | 0/3 | 3/3 | invented its own JSON shape every time; the repo's notebook doc points at the existing run-file format | capability: reading the repo for the missing requirement |
| `hb-incidents-11` (B) | 0/3 | 2/3 | fixed the month bucketing but left `started_at` in its original offset, against the store's documented "aware UTC" contract | capability: root cause vs symptom |
| `ha-claims-audit-04` (A) | 1/3 | 3/3 | one claim verdict off per failed trial (a different claim each time) | judgement on a verdict task; real but soft |
| `hb-money-ledger-01` (B) | 1/3 | 2/3 | every failed cell, Opus's included, failed the same test: `from_dict({"version": 3})` must raise `ValueError`, and implementations that read fields before checking the version raise `KeyError` | **borderline grader strictness** |
| `hb-config-layers-09` (B) | 1/3 | 3/3 | `load_config(project=None)` did not fall back to `./ledger.ini`; the brief states the default in the layer list without saying whether it belongs to `load_config` or the CLI | **borderline spec ambiguity** |

Two of the five are clean capability gaps, one is a soft judgement gap, and two
hang on a grader or brief choice. Read the true count of frontier-only work as
2 to 5 of 55, not 5.

### Grader corrections

Two rounds, both recorded in the task-set note with reasons: four tasks had a
defective test, and 16 cells that failed **only** the defective test were
re-graded as passes (originals kept in `outcomes.pre-regrade.jsonl` and in
each row's `regrade` field). Every correction accepts an extra defensible
reading or drops a rule the repo never stated; none made a grade stricter.
Before re-grading the run stood at Sonnet 91/120 and Opus 101/120; after, 99
and 109. The hard set is the same five tasks before and after. One more task (`ha-verify-suite-16`) has an over-strict grader but
could not be re-graded and stays unsolved; it is a defect, not a hard task.

## Reference values on the pooled set

Computed arms from the two calibration runs, on the 52 tasks that are not
unsolved (`model_routing.dispatch.exp06`, 95% task-clustered intervals):

| arm | completion | cost per completed task | vs plain Sonnet | vs plain Opus |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| plain Sonnet 5.5 | 91% [84, 97] | $0.277 [$0.236, $0.324] | 1.00× | 0.56× |
| plain Opus 5.5 | 98% [96, 100] | $0.497 [$0.430, $0.568] | 1.79× | 1.00× |
| perfect Sonnet→Opus trigger (rerun on Opus exactly when Sonnet failed, free) | 99% [97, 100] | $0.317 [$0.250, $0.395] | 1.14× | 0.64× |

This is the ceiling any ladder could reach on this work: about 8 points of
completion for 14% more cost than plain Sonnet, with a trigger that is
perfect and costs nothing. On the 45 easy tasks a perfect trigger never fires
and costs exactly plain Sonnet. On the hard stratum it lifts completion from
20% to 87% at $1.07 per completed task (Sonnet $1.77, Opus $0.87), on 5 tasks.

## What this closes

The finding is now: **in long single-session coding tasks, harvested real
handoffs, long-horizon multi-package work, ambiguous briefs and read-only
investigation, Opus 5.5 does work Sonnet 5.5 cannot on at most 5 of 55 tasks,
and a perfect escalation trigger would buy about 8 points of completion for
14% more cost.** By the v0.3 stopping rule exp06 stops at Stage 0. The ladder
is not revised to v0.4 or registered, and the confirmatory ladder is not run.
What remains measurable without hard work was measured in the first batch:
the ladder's idle cost (`../results.md`, Section 4).

exp07 is re-scoped to the one question the idle-cost data left open, whether a
cheap external gate can suppress unjustified escalation requests
(`../../exp07-jev-gate/preregistration.md`).
