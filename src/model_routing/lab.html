ⓘ What does each track test?

Adoption compares every single-model baseline, strongest model at low effort, free heuristic routing, a paid small-model classifier, and a confidence cascade. Each vendor runs separately. Research adds difficulty-label routing and an answer-key cascade as privileged reference strategies. These references cannot establish deployable savings.

The bundled workload uses deterministic checks. A passing rewrite or synthesis check does not establish human usefulness. Before a leadership decision, use representative work and blinded human review against a written rubric.

Quality and savings criteria

Compare matched task/trial pairs with all-strong. A 0.02 loss means two percentage points. Incomplete runs cannot pass the gate. Results are descriptive; repeated trials on the same tasks are not independent evidence.

ⓘ Cost, cache, and native product limits

Estimates use guessed output lengths, not a spending guarantee. The budget stops execution after a completed call, so the last call can exceed it. CLI token costs are a proxy, not your company’s invoice. This platform does not configure routing inside Cowork or ChatGPT Work. Caches may carry between strategies; a five-minute wait does not expire a one-hour cache.
