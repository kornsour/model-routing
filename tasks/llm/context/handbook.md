# Northwind Ridge Consulting — Employee Handbook (excerpt, revision 2026-07)

This excerpt is fictional reference material used as shared context for
routing experiments. It is written to be long enough to exceed every model's
minimum cacheable prefix and dense enough that questions about it have single
correct answers.

## 1. Employment basics

Northwind Ridge Consulting ("Northwind") employs roughly 640 people across
four offices: Denver (headquarters), New York, Austin, and London. Employees
are classified as exempt or non-exempt under applicable wage law. Non-exempt
employees are paid overtime at 1.5 times their regular hourly rate for hours
worked beyond 40 in a work week; overtime must be approved in advance by the
employee's manager. Exempt employees are not eligible for overtime pay.

Pay is issued semi-monthly, on the 15th and on the last calendar day of each
month. When a pay date falls on a weekend or bank holiday, pay is issued on the
preceding business day. Pay statements are available in the People Portal.

New hires complete a 90-day introductory period. During the introductory
period, employees accrue paid time off and sick leave normally but may not
take more than three consecutive days of paid time off without written
approval from a director.

## 2. Work location and hours

Northwind is a hybrid workplace. Employees assigned to an office are expected
to work from that office at least three days per week, one of which must be
Tuesday. Fully remote roles are approved by the Chief People Officer and are
noted in the offer letter; remote employees must live within the country of
their assigned legal entity.

Core hours are 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. in each employee's local time zone.
During core hours, employees are expected to be reachable and available for
meetings. Outside core hours, teams set their own norms. Meetings that span
offices should be scheduled inside the overlap of every participant's core
hours; if no overlap exists, the organizer rotates the inconvenience between
locations rather than always favoring headquarters.

Internal messages (chat and email) should receive a response within one
business day. Client-facing messages follow the engagement's service-level
agreement, which is documented in the engagement charter.

## 3. Paid time off

Regular full-time employees accrue paid time off (PTO) at 1.5 days per month
of service, for a total of 18 days per year. Accrual is credited on the last
day of each month. Part-time employees accrue proportionally to their
scheduled hours.

Unused PTO carries over from one calendar year to the next, subject to a
carryover cap of 10 days. Any balance above 10 days on December 31 is
forfeited; it is not paid out. Upon separation, accrued and unused PTO is paid
out where required by local law; elsewhere it is forfeited.

PTO requests of three days or fewer should be submitted at least three
business days in advance. Requests of more than three consecutive days must
be submitted at least two weeks in advance. Managers approve PTO in the People
Portal; approval is not guaranteed during a client go-live or during the last
two weeks of a fiscal quarter.

## 4. Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year, credited in
full on January 1 (prorated for mid-year hires). Sick leave is separate from
PTO and does not carry over. Sick leave may be used for the employee's own
illness or medical appointments, or to care for an immediate family member.
Absences of more than three consecutive sick days require a note from a
healthcare provider.

## 5. Company holidays

Northwind observes eleven paid holidays per year in the United States:
New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day,
Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the day after
Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day. The London office observes the
eight bank holidays of England and Wales plus Christmas Eve, for a total of
nine. When a U.S. holiday falls on a Saturday it is observed the preceding
Friday; when it falls on a Sunday it is observed the following Monday.

## 6. Other leave

- **Parental leave.** Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave;
  secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks. Leave may be taken within 12 months
  of birth or placement and must be taken in blocks of at least two weeks.
- **Bereavement.** Up to 5 paid days for the death of an immediate family
  member and up to 2 paid days for an extended family member.
- **Jury duty.** Paid for up to 10 business days per calendar year. Employees
  keep any juror fees. Time beyond 10 days is unpaid unless PTO is used.
- **Volunteering.** Each employee may use up to 16 hours per year of paid
  time for volunteering with a registered nonprofit, approved by a manager.
- **Military leave.** Provided in accordance with applicable law.

## 7. Business travel and expenses

All travel must be booked through TripDesk, the company travel tool, unless
TripDesk is unavailable and the booking is documented in the expense report.

- **Air.** Economy class is required for flights with a scheduled duration of
  under six hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of six hours or
  more. Business class requires approval from a vice president and is
  generally limited to flights of ten hours or more.
- **Lodging.** Hotel rooms are capped at $250 per night. The cap rises to the
  conference hotel's group rate when the employee is attending a conference
  and stays at the conference hotel. Any rate above the cap requires
  written manager approval before booking.
- **Meals.** Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to $75 per day for
  domestic travel and $110 per day for international travel. Alcohol is not
  reimbursable except at approved client entertainment events.
- **Ground transportation.** Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at $0.67 per
  mile. Rideshare and taxis are reimbursed at cost. Rental cars should be
  mid-size or smaller; insurance offered by the rental company should be
  declined because the corporate policy covers rentals.
- **Receipts.** Itemized receipts are required for any single expense of $25
  or more. Expenses without a required receipt are not reimbursed.
- **Deadlines.** Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the
  expense date. Reports submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed.
- **Approvals.** Reports under $500 are approved by the direct manager; $500
  to $5,000 by a director; above $5,000 by a vice president.

Client-billable expenses follow the client's policy where it is stricter than
Northwind's; the stricter rule always applies.

## 8. Equipment and home office

Each employee is issued a laptop, which is refreshed every 36 months or when
it fails, whichever is sooner. Employees receive a one-time home office
stipend of $1,500 within their first 60 days, and an accessories allowance of
$300 per calendar year for peripherals such as keyboards, mice, headsets, and
cables. Monitors are provided by the company for office desks; one monitor
per remote employee is covered by the home office stipend.

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours.
Personal devices used for work must be enrolled in device management and use
full-disk encryption.

## 9. Information security

Multi-factor authentication is required on all company accounts. Passwords
must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180 days; passphrases are
encouraged. Client data may be stored only in approved systems listed in the
Data Handling Standard; storing client data on personal devices or personal
cloud accounts is prohibited. Suspected phishing should be reported using the
"Report" button in the mail client; suspected incidents should be reported to
security@northwind.example within one hour of discovery.

## 10. Performance and compensation

Performance reviews occur twice a year, in March and September. Compensation
adjustments take effect on April 1 following the March review. Promotions may
be awarded in either cycle. Each employee maintains a set of goals in the
People Portal, reviewed at least monthly with their manager.

Northwind pays a referral bonus of $2,500 for a referred candidate who is
hired into a full-time role and completes 90 days of employment. Referrals
for roles at the director level or above pay $5,000 under the same
conditions. Recruiting and People team members are not eligible.

## 11. Benefits

- **Retirement.** Northwind matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4%
  of eligible pay. Matching contributions vest 25% per year of service and are
  fully vested after four years.
- **Health.** Medical, dental, and vision plans are offered. Open enrollment
  runs November 1 through November 15 each year; elections take effect on
  January 1. Qualifying life events allow changes within 30 days of the event.
- **Tuition reimbursement.** Up to $5,250 per calendar year for courses at an
  accredited institution that are relevant to the employee's role or a
  plausible next role. A grade of B or better (or "pass" for pass/fail
  courses) is required. Employees who leave voluntarily within 12 months of a
  reimbursement repay it in full.
- **Conferences and training.** Each employee has a professional development
  budget of $3,000 per calendar year, which includes registration, travel,
  and lodging for conferences. Unused budget does not carry over.
- **Wellness.** A $50 per month wellness reimbursement covers gym
  memberships, fitness classes, and mindfulness apps.

## 12. Conduct

Northwind expects professional, respectful conduct with colleagues, clients,
and vendors. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are prohibited.
Concerns may be raised with a manager, with the People team, or anonymously
through the ethics line at ethics.northwind.example. Gifts from vendors or
clients valued over $100 must be declined or reported to Compliance within
five business days. Employees may not hold outside employment with a
competitor or a client without written approval from the Chief People Officer.

## 14. Acknowledgment

This handbook is not a contract of employment and may be revised at any time.
Where local law is more generous than a policy described here, local law
applies. Employees acknowledge the handbook annually in the People Portal.

**London.** 20 Fenchurch Street, Level 12. Badge access 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.

may be reserved up to 14 days in advance in the workplace app; reservations

## Appendix B. Expense worked examples

**Example 1 — client site day trip.** An Austin employee drives 84 miles
round trip to a client, buys lunch for $22 and dinner for $41, and pays a $12
parking fee at the client's garage. Mileage is 84 × $0.67 = $56.28. Meals
total $63, under the $75 domestic cap, so $63 is reimbursed. Parking is
reimbursed at cost; because the fee is under $25 no receipt is required,
although one is encouraged. Total reimbursement: $131.28. The report is under
$500, so the direct manager approves it.

**Example 2 — conference.** A New York employee attends a three-day
conference in Chicago and stays at the conference hotel at the group rate of
$289 per night. Because it is the conference hotel, the $250 cap does not
apply and the full $289 per night is reimbursable. Registration, airfare, and
lodging are charged against the employee's $3,000 professional development
budget; meals are reimbursed against the $75 domestic daily cap and are not
charged to the development budget.

**Example 3 — late submission.** An employee submits a $140 rideshare expense
71 days after the ride. Because more than 60 days have passed, the expense is
not reimbursed, regardless of the receipt.

**Example 4 — international meals.** A Denver employee traveling in Germany
spends €58 on meals in a day, converted at $63. The international cap is $110,
so $63 is reimbursed. A glass of wine with dinner is excluded unless the
dinner was an approved client entertainment event.

## Appendix C. Frequently asked questions

**Can I take PTO before I have accrued it?** Managers may approve up to five
days of negative PTO balance for employees past the introductory period. A
negative balance at separation is deducted from the final paycheck where the
law allows.

**Does PTO accrue while I am on parental leave?** Yes. PTO and sick leave
continue to accrue during paid parental leave.

**What if a holiday falls during my PTO?** The holiday is not deducted from
your PTO balance.

**Can I combine the accessories allowance across years?** No. The $300
accessories allowance resets each January 1 and does not accumulate.

**How is the tuition reimbursement paid?** After the course ends, submit the
grade report and receipt in the People Portal. Reimbursement appears on the
next regular pay date after approval. The $5,250 annual limit is applied by
the date of reimbursement, not the date the course started.

**What counts as a "business day"?** Monday through Friday, excluding company
holidays observed in the employee's office.

**How do I dispute an expense denial?** Reply in the expense tool within 10
business days with the additional documentation. Disputes are reviewed by
Finance within 5 business days.

**Who approves remote work from another country?** Working from outside the
country of your legal entity for more than 14 days in a calendar year
requires approval from the Chief People Officer and from Tax, because it can
create payroll and tax obligations for the company.

**How much notice is required to change my benefits after a life event?**
Thirty days from the qualifying event, with documentation.

## Appendix D. Meeting and communication norms

Northwind's default meeting length is 25 minutes for short meetings and 50
minutes for long ones, leaving time between back-to-back meetings. Every
meeting invitation should include an agenda; invitations without an agenda
may be declined. Recurring meetings are reviewed each quarter and cancelled
if fewer than half of the invitees attend on average.

Written decisions belong in the decision log of the relevant project, not in
chat threads. Client deliverables are reviewed by a second consultant before
they are sent; the reviewer is named in the engagement charter. Use the
"urgent" flag in chat only for issues that block a client or a go-live.

Chat channels use the following prefixes: `proj-` for engagements, `team-`
for internal teams, `help-` for support channels, and `social-` for
non-work channels. Announcements from leadership are posted in
`#announcements`, which is read-only.

## Appendix E. Glossary

- **Engagement charter** — the document that defines a client engagement's
  scope, staffing, service-level agreement, reviewer, and billing terms.
- **Go-live** — the planned date a client system or process enters
  production use; PTO approval is restricted in the two weeks before it.
- **Introductory period** — the first 90 days of employment.
- **People Portal** — the HR system used for pay statements, PTO, goals,
  benefits elections, and handbook acknowledgment.
- **TripDesk** — the company travel booking tool.
- **Core hours** — 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. local time, when employees are
  expected to be reachable.
- **Qualifying life event** — marriage, birth or adoption, loss of other
  coverage, or a change in a dependent's status, which allows benefit changes
  outside open enrollment within 30 days.
